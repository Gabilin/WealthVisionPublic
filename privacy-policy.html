Privacy Policy

Updated 31 October 2026

WealthVision was built on a simple promise: your money is nobody's business but yours. This policy explains, in plain language, what that means. The short version is that we do not collect, transmit, or sell your app data. Saved plans remain on your device, not on a WealthVision server.

Information We Collect

None. WealthVision does not require an account and does not ask for personally identifying information. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, location, or contacts.

Saved goals, logged contributions, payday plans, learning progress, reminder preferences, and Kid Mode details are kept only in local storage on your device. Growth simulations and scenario comparisons run locally, but their inputs and results are not saved between sessions. We cannot access the financial numbers you enter.

How Your Data Is Stored

  • All app data lives locally on your device using standard on-device storage.
  • WealthVision has no developer-operated backend or cloud-sync service for your app data.
  • You can remove local data by clearing the app's data or uninstalling it. Device backups and restoration are controlled by your operating system and its settings.

What We Do Not Do

  • We do not use analytics or telemetry services.
  • We do not embed advertising networks or third-party trackers.
  • We do not connect to your bank, hold funds, or process transactions of any kind.
  • We do not build profiles, run experiments on you, or share data with partners.

Network Access

WealthVision is designed to run fully offline. Currency symbols and number formats are read from your device's locale settings with no network request. The app makes no calls to fetch prices, rates, or any external data.

Downloading or updating the app, buying Pro, and restoring a purchase use Apple or Google services and require an internet connection. Optional reminders use local device notifications.

Children's Privacy (Kid Mode)

Kid Mode is a local, offline savings experience for children. Like the rest of WealthVision, it sends no child data to us. A child's nickname, allowance choices, and savings progress can be stored locally. There is no sign-in, public child profile, advertising network, or bank connection. Parents manage the experience in the app.

Purchases

WealthVision offers a single, optional, one-time purchase to unlock advanced features. Payments are handled entirely by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We never see or store your payment details. Your purchase is validated by the store, and their respective privacy policies govern that transaction.

Purchases belong to the store account used to buy Pro. Apple and Google purchases are separate.

This Website

This website uses no analytics or email collection forms. The hosting provider receives the technical information needed to deliver a page, such as your IP address and browser request. Website fonts and styling are loaded from Google Fonts and a content-delivery network; their providers receive those browser requests. This is separate from the app's locally stored financial information.

Following a store link takes you to Apple or Google, where that provider's privacy policy applies. If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include.

Educational Disclaimer

WealthVision is an educational and planning tool, not financial advice. All projections are illustrative and based on historical averages. Past performance does not guarantee future results.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new effective date. Because the app collects nothing, we do not anticipate material changes to how your data is handled.
