# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Existing static HTML site, deployed at https://www.wealthvision.space/.

## Users

Privacy-conscious adults learning to plan a goal, organize payday, or understand
investing. Parents can explore the app's Kid Mode for allowance and savings.

## Product Purpose

WealthVision is an offline money coach and goal planner for iOS and Android.
The website helps visitors understand the app and download it from their store.

## Positioning

Short visual lessons connect to practical planning tools. No accounts, bank
linking, ads, or tracking. Financial plans remain on the user's device.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The app makes and remembers plans; it never moves money.
- Projections are illustrations, not promises or financial advice.
- A useful Free tier and optional one-time Pro unlock, not a subscription.
- The app stores display the current local price and handle purchases.
- Purchases belong to the store account; no cross-store entitlement promise.
- Preserve static deployment and the homepage, pricing, and privacy routes.
- Remove waitlist and Product Hunt promotion, as confirmed by the owner.

## Brand Commitments

Name: WealthVision. Tagline: Watch your future unfold.
Voice: plain-spoken, honest, judgment-free. The owner rejected the replacement
visual direction and explicitly requested the previous design with updated
content and links. Preserve the checked-out dark styling, typography, layout,
and interactive browser demonstrations; do not introduce a new visual world.

## Evidence on Hand

The neighboring WealthVision Flutter repository owns app behavior, current store
IDs, and real simulator screenshots. Use its current English App Store captures.
There are no verified ratings, testimonials, user counts, or press endorsements;
do not invent them.

## Product Principles

1. Lead with actual app screens and one useful money decision.
2. Make both store destinations easy to find.
3. Explain privacy through concrete data handling, not vague assurances.
4. Keep pricing and projection limits honest.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Responsive desktop and mobile layouts, keyboard-operable navigation and controls,
visible focus, readable contrast, and reduced-motion support.
